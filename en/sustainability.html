From Story to Action

A Story Made Real, Not Just Told

On the About page, we talked about why the energy transition matters. Here, we talk about what's actually being done.

Indonesia is committed to reaching Net Zero Emission by 2060. A commitment that big can't stay a slogan — it has to come down to small things you can touch, wear, and take home. Speed Land Series takes the concrete road: every production decision, every corner of race day, is designed so the running is fast but the impact stays clean.

It isn't about waiting for big change to come from the top. It's about thousands of small steps — a runner choosing to bring an old jersey, an organizer counting a carbon footprint, a partner recycling instead of discarding. Each one is small on its own; put together, that's how a nation moves toward clean energy.

Jersey of Light

One Jersey, the Light of Three Cities

This jersey is born from three cities — representing the energy of runners in Jakarta, Bali, and Yogyakarta. Not just a uniform, but proof that a single movement can unite steps from far-apart corners.

How it works

Used jerseys from the PLN tour across the three cities are collected, then recycled into new eco-friendly jerseys. The process is done together with apparel partner MILLS, under the #SayaPilihBumi campaign — so fabric that has already made one journey can begin its next.
