Venue Access

Venue Access Information

Important things to know before arriving at Taman Wisata Candi Prambanan.

Address
Jl. Raya Solo - Yogyakarta, Kranggan, Bokoharjo, Kec. Prambanan, Kabupaten Sleman, DIY 55571
BIB = Entry Ticket
Your BIB serves as an entry ticket to the Prambanan Temple complex on race day. No separate ticket purchase is required.
Parking
Parking is available at the Prambanan Temple complex. Participants driving their own vehicles are advised to arrive before 04:00 WIB.
Companions / Supporters
Companions who wish to enter the venue area must purchase Prambanan Temple entry tickets online through the official website.
