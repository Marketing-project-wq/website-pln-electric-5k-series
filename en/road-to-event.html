About the Program

Not Just a Meet-Up — Training With Purpose

Power Up The Run is a community warm-up program on the way to race day — where you build speed together, not alone.

In collaboration with 20FIT Sport Clinic, every session is designed to raise your performance step by step. This isn't just a casual group jog — there's structure behind it, so each month you show up stronger than the last.

What you build in every session

  • Speed training — dedicated speed work to build endurance and push your target pace higher.
  • Strength — training that supports your body so it can carry a full 5K without running out of gas.
  • Performance — a measured approach with 20FIT Sport Clinic so your progress is real, not just a feeling.
  • Recovery — recovery sessions to keep your body ready to bounce back, instead of breaking down before race day.

Fast is trained, not accidental. Show up every month, and feel the difference when the start line opens.
