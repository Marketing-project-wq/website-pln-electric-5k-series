Pickup Requirements

What You Need to Bring

Race packs can be picked up in person or by a representative. Expand each option below for the requirements.

Come to the Race Pack Collection venue in person with:

  1. Valid photo ID (KTP / driver's licence / passport)
  2. Registration confirmation email (printed or digital)
  3. Participants under 17 must bring a signed Parental Consent Letter
Download Parental Consent Letter

If you cannot attend, a representative may collect your race pack with:

  1. Printed registration confirmation email
  2. Printed and signed Power of Attorney letter
  3. Original photo IDs of both the race-pack owner & the representative

Limit: a maximum of 3 race packs per representative per queue.
