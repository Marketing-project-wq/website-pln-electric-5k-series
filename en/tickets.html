Ticket Timeline

Act Early. Save More.

Prices step up twice through August. Lock in your spot now and save the rest for race day.

  1. From 14 August — Super Early Bird. The lowest price you'll see all series. Quota's tight, so early movers win.
  2. From 18 August — Early Bird. Missed Super Early Bird? There's still a great price waiting for you.
  3. From 28 August — General Sales. Standard pricing, open to everyone — but every city still has a runner cap, so don't leave it too late.

Running with a community or club? Ask about Community Price — a dedicated rate that runs independently of the tiers above.

How to Register

Four Steps Through PLN Mobile

All registration happens inside the PLN Mobile app. There's no form on this page — just follow these steps.

  1. Download or update the PLN Mobile app on your phone.
  2. Open the Events menu.
  3. Select PLN Mobile Electric 5K Series, then choose your city.
  4. Pick your ticket and complete payment.

Before You Register

All registration is through the PLN Mobile app: download or update the app, open the Events menu, select PLN Mobile Electric 5K Series and your city, then pick a ticket and pay.

Just one category: 5K. It's the same across all three cities, open to men and women aged over 10.

The COT is 40 minutes from the 6 AM start. Make sure you complete the 5K within that window.
