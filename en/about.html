About the Series

Every Stride Is Part of the Energy Transition

Indonesia is committed to reaching Net Zero Emission by 2060. But the energy transition can't happen in power plants alone — it has to live in the awareness of 280 million people. That's where Electric 5K Series begins: every stride is a symbol of the energy that powers the earth. You run fast, but the impact stays clean.

The Background

The Energy That Powers a Nation

PLN carries a monumental task: steering Indonesia toward Net Zero Emission by 2060. This is not simply swapping coal plants for renewables. It's about changing how an entire nation sees energy — from something taken for granted to something we look after together.

And here lies the heart of it: the energy transition will never be enough if it only happens in a power plant's control room. It has to live in the awareness of 280 million people. Every small decision — switching off a light, choosing clean transport, valuing the electricity that flows into our homes — is part of the same transition.

Why through running?

Because running is the most honest language of energy. Nothing moves you but your own body. Every stride is energy you burn to move forward — clean, measurable, leaving no trace. Electric 5K Series takes that metaphor and brings it to the streets of three cities: the runner as a symbol of the energy that powers a nation.

The running is fast — this is a 5K series built for speed. But the impact stays clean: performance and the environment are not two opposing things, but one and the same movement.

This spirit was first framed under the name “Power Up The Nation” — reigniting the nation's energy through a shared movement. In the final identity, the call became more personal and direct to every runner: Power Your Speed.
