Landstrike

200 Meters. Pure Speed.

The road to race day starts here. Landstrike is the series' 200 m speed test — one flat-out sprint in each host city, with its own live leaderboard powered by the same timing system as the main 5K.

The Warm-Up

A Sprint on the Road to 5K

Before the full 5K arrives in each city, Landstrike brings runners together for a short, explosive test: 200 metres, no pacing, just top speed. It's the perfect way to try the timing tech, feel the start line, and land on the leaderboard ahead of the main event.
