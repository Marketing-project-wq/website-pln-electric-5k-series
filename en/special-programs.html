For Those Chasing Speed

Speed 100

This is the reward for the ones who truly flew. In every city, the 100 fastest finishers — 50 men and 50 women, ranks 1 through 50 in each category — receive an exclusive windbreaker you can't buy anywhere.

This windbreaker isn't just merch. It's a badge of honour: proof you were among the hundred fastest in that city. You wear it, and people know exactly how quickly your legs devoured five kilometers.

Fast on the course, clean in impact — exactly the spirit of Power Your Speed. Every second you shave off is pure energy from your own body.

For Those Chasing the Moment

Land Strike

At the middle of the route sits a 200-metre sprint zone, marked by a balloon gate and a carpet running the length of the zone. The moment you enter it, automatic chip timing records your time separately from your 5K time.

No extra registration. No extra fee. Every runner who passes through is automatically in. All you decide is whether to conserve your energy — or empty the tank across those 200 metres.

The fastest time in the sprint zone claims the Sprint King and Sprint Queen titles for that city. One burst of power, one moment, one crown.
