Race Terms

What's Confirmed

The terms below apply to all participants across the three cities — Jakarta, Yogyakarta, and Bali.

Category & distance

There is a single category: 5K. It is exactly the same in every city. There are no other distance categories.

Cut-Off Time (COT)

The cut-off time (COT) is 40 minutes from the start. Participants who complete the 5K within this window are recorded as official finishers.

Age requirement

The race is open to both men and women aged over 10 years old.

For participants under 18, running events typically require parental or guardian consent or supervision. The official terms for this are still being finalized and will be announced on this page before registration closes.

Land Strike

All 5K participants are automatically entered in Land Strike — no extra registration and no additional fee. Your time in the sprint zone is recorded separately by chip timing. Claiming the Sprint King and Sprint Queen titles is subject to its own terms and conditions, based on chip-timing records.
