Registration

Points 13–18

  1. The Race registration process signifies that the Participant has read, understood, and agreed to the entire contents of the Rules & Regulations.
  2. Each Participant is permitted to register only once for this Race.
  3. Registration is done online through the official PLN Mobile app. Registering through that channel is deemed a form of agreement to these Rules & Regulations.
  4. A payment-confirmation email together with a registration identity number will be sent once the system has successfully verified the Participant's payment.
  5. The Organizer reserves the right to close registration before the stated deadline, including if the participant quota is filled earlier than expected.
  6. Participants must fill in true, accurate, current, and complete data on the registration form, and are fully responsible for the accuracy of that data. The Organizer may contact the Participant at any time via email, and any notice sent to the registered email address is deemed to have been received by the Participant.

Participation

Points 19–49

  1. Participants must complete the entire 5K route, from the official start line to the official finish line, within the Cut Off Time set by the Organizer. Determination of official timing rests entirely with the Organizer.
  2. The Cut Off Time for the 5K category is set at 40 (forty) minutes.
  3. The BIB number serves as the Participant's personal identity and may not be exchanged or transferred to any other party.
  4. Selling or transferring a registration slot/BIB number to another party is prohibited; violating this results in disqualification and/or a ban from taking part in similar events in the future.
  5. The BIB number is handed over after the Participant shows proof of registration and an identity card, and must be worn clearly visible throughout the Race. The Organizer is authorized to withhold the race pack or BIB number if indications of a violation such as data falsification are found.
  6. The start corral will close as soon as the 5K category start signal is sounded. Participants not yet in the corral at that time are not permitted to start and will be recorded with a DNS (Did Not Start) status.
  7. Unsportsmanlike conduct, including obstructing or blocking the path of other Participants along the route, will result in automatic disqualification.
  8. Drink stations are provided along the race route as well as in the finish-line area.
  9. Motor vehicles, bicycles, roller skates, skateboards, strollers, wheelchairs, or other unofficial mobility aids are not permitted on the Race route.
  10. Pets, whether leashed or not, are prohibited from being brought into the Race route area.
  11. Participants are prohibited from wearing gear that risks endangering themselves or other Participants, including covering the face while running.
  12. The attire worn by Participants must comply with the standards of propriety for a sporting event and must not disturb the comfort of fellow Participants or spectators.
  13. Using clothing as a means of political or religious campaigning, or as certain promotional/advertising material, is not permitted.
  14. Participants are prohibited from carrying attributes that could be misinterpreted as official signals, confusing other Participants or the Organizer, or from using sound-producing devices that could disturb the comfort of other Participants.
  15. Activities that could hinder the smooth running of the Race — such as dancing, performing in public, playing musical instruments, or standing in the start/finish area or along the route — are not permitted.
  16. Any form of fundraising carried out by a Participant while taking part in the PLN Mobile Electric 5K Series is the personal responsibility of the Participant concerned, not the responsibility of the Organizer.
  17. Participants are prohibited from obstructing pedestrian crossings or emergency-vehicle lanes at certain designated points.
  18. Timing boards will be installed in the start and finish areas as a reference for Participants.
  19. The Organizer may establish a number of Cut Off Points along the 5K route. Details of the location and time of each point will be communicated through a separate official announcement.
  20. Participants who do not meet the Cut Off Time and/or Cut Off Point, including disqualified Participants, are declared not to have finished the Race and are not entitled to a finisher medal.
  21. Non-compliance with the instructions of the Organizer or officials that disrupts the smooth running of the Race may be grounds for disqualifying a Participant.
  22. The Organizer is authorized to end the participation of a Participant who behaves inappropriately during the Race and to disqualify them.
  23. Carrying items that could potentially disrupt the safety or smooth progress of other Participants is a prohibited act.
  24. Security along the route is the responsibility of the police and related agencies, while medical services are provided by the Organizer in cooperation with local hospitals and medical personnel. Medical personnel are authorized to stop a Participant deemed medically unfit to continue the Race.
  25. Each Participant receives a chipped BIB number to record their time automatically. Checkpoints will be placed at several parts of the route to ensure Participants follow the official course; Participants who leave the official course will be disqualified.
  26. The Race winner (the "Winner") must wear their own BIB number and timing chip, and show official proof of identity to obtain the prize.
  27. A Winner who cannot show valid proof of identity may be disqualified and lose their entitlement to the prize.
  28. The Organizer may update the contents of the Rules & Regulations at any time at its own discretion without prior notice. Participants are therefore advised to review the Rules & Regulations each time they access the PLN Mobile app and/or the official PLN Mobile Electric 5K Series website.

Race Pack

Points 50–55

  1. The race pack is not shipped to the Participant's address and cannot be collected on Race Day.
  2. If one participant's name is found registered under two different entries, the Organizer reserves the right to withhold all related BIB numbers and race packs, and to disqualify the Participant concerned.
  3. Race-pack collection may be delegated to another party (a "Race Pack Collector") by attaching a printed letter of authorization, the race-pack collection ticket email, and the original identity documents of both the authorizer and the authorized party in printed form. The letter-of-authorization template can be downloaded via the official link here.
  4. One Race Pack Collector is permitted to collect a maximum of 3 (three) race packs in a single queue.
  5. The committee is authorized to not hand over the race pack and all its contents (including the BIB number), and to disqualify the Participant from the Race, if indications of a violation such as duplicate registration, identity falsification, or other similar conditions are found.
  6. The schedule and location of race-pack collection, including BIB number information, will be communicated further via an official email from the Organizer.

Prizes

Points 56–61

  1. Winners are determined based on the Participants who reach the finish line with the fastest recorded time (gun time).
  2. Winners must wear the BIB number and timing chip registered under their own name, and show official proof of identity to obtain the prize.
  3. A Winner who cannot show valid official identity during verification will be disqualified and lose their entitlement to the championship title and the prize.
  4. Prospective Winners must report to the verification tent no later than 30 (thirty) minutes after finishing the Race. Absence without notifying the committee may result in disqualification, unless the Prospective Winner is under the care of the medical team.
  5. Prizes in the form of cash are subject to tax in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations, and that tax obligation is the personal responsibility of each Winner.
  6. Prize disbursement is processed no later than 45 (forty-five) working days from Race Day. Winners must ensure the accuracy of their bank account details, address, and other information requested by the Organizer; data errors may slow the disbursement process and are the full responsibility of the Winner concerned.
